Case No.070

BlackSuit Ransomware / KCKPD / Wired & KCUR (Kansas City Police Misconduct List)

Dual-Use Research · 2024–2025

Following a 2024 ransomware breach at the Kansas City, Kansas Police Department, leaked records exposed an internal list of officers whose credibility was questioned. Journalists used the stolen archive to report on police misconduct and public accountability.

Journalist / activist / independent
Good faith (intended public benefit)
Media-first
No charges filed

Key ethical dilemmas

(i) Is it ethical for journalists and researchers to analyze and publish findings from data stolen by ransomware criminals, when the findings expose genuine government misconduct?; (ii) Does restricting access to journalists, researchers, and defense attorneys adequately mitigate the harms of publishing stolen government data?; (iii) Does the public interest in police accountability ever justify using data obtained by criminal actors?; (iv) What is the difference between this and the Hacking Team case — does the target's identity (law enforcement vs. surveillance vendor) change the ethical analysis?
